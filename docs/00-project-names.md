# Project Name — Options & Recommendation

---

## The Naming Brief

This product is not a code generator.
It's not another vibe coding tool.
It's the **product intelligence layer** — the thinking system that sits upstream of all coding tools.

The name must communicate: intelligence, structure, building, clarity.
It must NOT sound like: a chatbot, a code tool, a design plugin.

---

## Name Options

---

### 🥇 BULUP — Recommended

**bulup.so** or **trybulup.io** or **usebulup.co**

> Bulup: a place where raw material is shaped into something real under pressure and skill.

- Short. One syllable. Memorable.
- Tactile — it implies craft, not automation
- "Forging a product" is a natural phrase
- Doesn't sound like a code tool or a design plugin
- Works as both noun and verb: "We bulupd this in Bulup."
- Tagline potential: *"Where products are made."* / *"Think. Shape. Ship."*

**Verdict:** Strong, clean, category-defining. This is the name.

---

### 🥈 CORTEX

**cortex.so** or **trycortex.io**

> Cortex: the outer layer of the brain. The thinking organ.

- Directly communicates "intelligence layer"
- Premium, technical-feeling
- Good for positioning as "the brain of your product"
- Tagline: *"The thinking layer between your idea and your code."*

**Verdict:** Strong second. Use if Bulup is taken or too generic in your market.

---

### 🥉 HELM

**helm.so** or **usehelm.io**

> Helm: the wheel that steers a ship. Control + direction.

- Communicates product direction and control
- Clean, minimal feel
- Works for "steer your product"
- Tagline: *"Take the helm."*

**Verdict:** Good. Slightly less distinctive. Has associations with Helm (Kubernetes) which could confuse dev audience.

---

### 4. STRATA

**strata.so** or **strataapp.io**

> Strata: layers of structure. Geological depth. Systematic thinking.

- Communicates layered product thinking
- Slightly abstract — requires more explanation
- Premium, architectural feel

**Verdict:** Good for a design-heavy audience. Less punchy as a brand.

---

### 5. FORMA

**forma.so** or **useforma.io**

> Forma: form, shape, structure.

- Elegant, minimal
- Design-forward
- Could feel too design-tool-adjacent (like a Figma competitor)

**Verdict:** Beautiful word. Risk of being confused with a design tool, not a product intelligence tool.

---

## Final Recommendation

# BULUP

Short. Strong. Tactile. No confusion.

Tagline options:
- *"Think before you build."*
- *"Where products are bulupd."*
- *"From idea to product. Without the chaos."*
- *"The product intelligence layer."*

Brand voice direction: Confident. Direct. No hype. Built for people who ship real things.

---

*All documents in this pack use BULUP as the product name. Swap if final name differs.*
